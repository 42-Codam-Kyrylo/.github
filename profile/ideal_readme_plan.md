## 👋 About Me & The 42 Pedagogy

I am a software engineering student at **Codam (42 Network)**. The 42 curriculum is unique: it has **no teachers, no classes, and no rigid schedule**. 
Instead, it relies entirely on **peer-to-peer learning** and rigorous **project-based evaluations**. 

Through this program, I have developed:
- 🧩 **First-principles thinking** by rewriting standard libraries (C) from scratch.
- 🤝 **Strong collaboration skills** through constant peer code reviews and team projects.
- 🚀 **Autonomy and resilience** by solving complex technical challenges independently.
